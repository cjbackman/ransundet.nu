title: "The Ransundet books"
---

The first Ransundet book was published in 1996 and tells the story of the village of Ransundet from its founding in 1825 up to 1996. It was written by Karl-Ivar Persson, Evert Amundsson and Lennart Persson. The books are in Swedish.

[Click here to download the first Ransundet book.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XM7r9QBSmndYvVpZU3zkPKHusnr2lOo6/view?usp=sharing){:target="\_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}

On behalf of the Ransundet village association, and to mark the village's 200th anniversary, a second book written by Joel Backman was published in 2025. Among other things, it explores old settlements, travel routes, emigration, immigration and recent history.
